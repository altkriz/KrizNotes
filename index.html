Designed for Privacy, Speed & Simplicity

Everything you need to write and secure your thoughts without the bloat.

🔒

100% Offline & Private

Zero internet connection required for note storage. All notes are saved locally on your device in an encrypted Room database. Your data never touches a remote server.

🛡️

Encrypted Note Vault

Lock your most sensitive notes behind a custom passcode or biometric authentication. Encrypted notes remain invisible until safely unlocked.

✍️

Rich Markdown Formatting

Full support for headers, bold, italics, code blocks, bullet points, checklists, and links. Live preview lets you format notes effortlessly.

📂

Import Text & Backup Files

Easily import individual or multiple .txt and .md files as notes. Export encrypted ZIP backups to keep your thoughts safe across devices.

🎨

Material Design 3 & AMOLED

Built with Jetpack Compose. Enjoy dynamic colors, customizable card corner radii, and a true-black Extreme AMOLED mode that saves battery.

👁️

Privacy Screen Protection

Optional screen protection obscures app previews in Android's recent apps view and blocks unauthorized screenshots while you're taking notes.

Your Privacy is Absolute

No telemetry, no behavioral profiles, no advertising SDKs, and zero cloud leaks. Read our complete privacy promise.
